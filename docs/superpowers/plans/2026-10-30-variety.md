# Variety Without Sounding Stupid: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ideas stop sounding copy-pasted and robotic, through four kinds of change: phrase variation, groove accents with ghost notes, a timing feel, and varied note lengths and voicings.
Every change stays small, follows a rule a producer would recognise, and is tied to energy and groove.

**Architecture:**
- **The engine** writes phrase variation, accents, ghost notes, note lengths and inversions into the idea's events, so validation, pins, refit and the display all see them.
- **The timing feel** is a performance layer. It is applied only when the idea becomes a playback pattern or a MIDI file, so every event in the idea stays on the grid.
- **Determinism:** each lane draws its new randomness from new fixed RNG slots of its own lane seed, so the same seed always gives the same idea.

**Tech Stack:** C++20, Catch2, CMake presets, md-cli.

**Spec:** the owner's request of 2026-10-29: "a bit more variety ... without making them sound stupid and too robotic", with all four kinds and the amount tied to energy and groove.

## Global Constraints

- The ticks are `kTicksPerQuarter = 480`. At 124 bpm one tick is about 1 ms.
- **Every idea still passes `validate`.** That means no bass clash, no bass onset on a downbeat except sustained, ranges kept, a monophonic bass and melody, and the melody leap rules.
- **Energy tiers** use engine energy 0 to 100:
  - **Low**, energy below 30: accents and timing feel only.
  - **Mid**, 30 to 60: low, plus the chords' bar-8 turnaround and the melody answer phrase.
  - **High**, above 60: mid, plus the bass fill in bar 8, ghost notes, the bar-4 breath, varied lengths and inversions.
- **Phrase positions.** Variation happens only in the last bar of each 4-bar phrase.
  - Bar 4 gets the "breath".
  - Bar 8, the last bar of the idea, gets the turnaround.
  - For 16-bar ideas, bars 8 and 16 get turnarounds and bars 4 and 12 get breaths.
  - For 4-bar ideas, only bar 4 varies, and it gets the turnaround.
- **Never louder or busier than the style.** Turnaround rhythms come from the same entry's own chord and melody rhythm cells, never invented.
- **The bass stays locked to the kick.** It has a timing offset of 0 at every setting.
- **Docs:** no em dash. Long Markdown has one sentence per line.
- **Commits:** never add Co-Authored-By or an agent name.
- **Before each commit:** dev tests, lint and format must pass.

## Musical rules

**Accents**
- These replace today's random humanize spread.
- The step offsets are added to the lane base velocity, by 16th step within the beat:
  - the beat (step % 4 == 0): +4
  - the offbeat 8th (step % 4 == 2): +8
  - the other 16ths: -8
- Add a seeded random spread of ±3 from the lane's existing humanize slot.
- Approach notes keep their 85% multiplier, applied before the accent.

**Ghost notes** (high tier only)
- **Chords:**
  - At most one per bar and at most 4 per idea of 8 bars, scaled for other lengths.
  - Each is the bar's current voicing, 1 sixteenth long, at 40% of the base velocity.
  - It goes on an odd 16th that is at least 2 steps from any chord hit.
  - Never in a turnaround bar.
- **Bass:**
  - At most 2 per 8 bars.
  - Each is the chord root an octave up or down, whichever is in range, 1 sixteenth long, at 40% velocity.
  - It goes on an empty odd 16th, never a downbeat, and never adjacent to another bass note.
  - Not on sustained or pedal patterns.

**Breath** (high tier, bar 4 of each 8)
- The chords drop their last hit in the bar.
- The melody drops its last note in the bar, if that leaves at least one note in the 2-bar phrase.

**Turnaround** (last bar of the idea, and bar 8 of 16)
- **Chords**, mid tier and up: the bar's rhythm is replaced by another of the entry's chord rhythm cells, drawn from a new chords slot, using that cell's bar 1 only.
- **Melody**, mid tier and up: bars 7 and 8 (the second half of the last 4-bar phrase) use another of the entry's melody rhythm cells, as an answer phrase. The pitches are drawn by the existing melody rules from a new melody slot.
- **Bass**, high tier only, and not on sustained or pedal:
  - The last 2 beats (steps 8 to 15 of the last bar) become a fill of 3 or 4 sixteenths on steps from {9, 10, 11, 13, 14}, plus the existing approach note on 15.
  - The fill walks scale tones toward the next root, which is the idea's first bass note.
  - Each note is 1 sixteenth long.
  - It must pass clash and downbeat validation. Redraw up to 8 times, then fall back to no fill.

**Lengths** (high tier)
- Chord hits of 2 sixteenths or fewer get a length of 70%, 85% or 100% of written, drawn per hit, with a minimum of 60 ticks.
- Longer hits stay as written.

**Inversions** (high tier)
- In bars 3 to 4 and 7 to 8, a chord that repeats the previous span's symbol may move to its next inversion.
- It only moves if the resulting voicing passes the existing voice-leading checks and keeps the top note inside the entry's `topNote` range.
- Otherwise it stays.
- For parallel entries, never.

**Timing feel** (performance layer, all tiers)
- Offsets are in ticks. The chords lay-back offset is 2, 5, 8 or 10 for groove straight, 54, 58 or 62.
- Melody: each note gets a seeded offset in ±(2 + groove index × 1.5), rounded. The groove index is 0 to 3.
- Bass: 0.
- An offset never moves a note before tick 0.
- A melody offset never makes a note overlap its neighbour. Clamp the note to the neighbour.
- The idea's end tick is unchanged, and a note that would cross it is shortened.

**RNG slots** (new ones only; existing slots keep their meaning)
- chords: 4 turnaround cell, 5 ghosts, 6 lengths, 7 inversions
- bass: 6 ghosts, 7 fill
- melody: 5 answer cell, 6 answer pitches, 7 timing feel
- chords and melody breath: no draw
