Three new layouts, same console look

Every control here works today. Nothing is left as an empty slot. Click the one closest to what you want, then tell me in the terminal what you like and what you'd change.

A. Mixer strips

Each part gets its own channel strip beside the display, like a small desk: colour stripe, level knob and mute. The feel knobs and transport share one tight row underneath.

B. Groovebox

Like a drum machine. The display runs the full width, a row of knobs sits below it, and a row of big keys along the bottom: one lit key per part to mute it, then play, stop and sprinkle.

C. Compact rack

A short, wide rack unit. The display is on the left and a grid of eight knobs on the right, with a single row of keys under the display. Smallest window, nothing wasted.
